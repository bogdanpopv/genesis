## Pain Point Description 
The major pain point identified within the community is the difficulty of finding people to play board games with. This issue stems from various factors such as the lack of a broader social group interested in board games, life circumstances that limit people's availability to play, and the nature of some games requiring a larger number of participants.

## Stakeholders 
The individuals affected by this pain point are the community members who are passionate about playing board games and desire to engage in these activities with others. This includes individuals who have access to a collection of board games but struggle to find participants to play with.

## Detailed Context 
This issue matters because playing board games is primarily a social activity that requires the participation of multiple individuals. The lack of participants can hinder the enjoyment and engagement of the community members. It also limits the variety and type of games that can be played, as some games require a larger number of participants for a fulfilling gameplay.
